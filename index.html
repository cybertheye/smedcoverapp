EP.00编辑器The editor

只填文字，样子交给风格。Just the words; the look does the rest.

「内容」面板管的就是字：频道、标题、副标题和角标。配色、版式、字效都由档案和风格决定，你只管这一期写什么。The Content sheet is where the words go: a channel, a title, a subtitle and a corner tag. Colours, layout and lettering come from the profile and the look; you only say what this episode is about.

  1. 频道Channel每个频道有自己的名称、主色和图形，封面的配色跟着频道走。Each channel has its own name, colour and graphic; the cover takes its colours from it.
  2. 标题Title输入框里的一行就是封面上的一行；*星号*包住的词显示为频道主色。One line here is one line on the cover; words in *asterisks* take the channel colour.
  3. 所有尺寸Every size每个输出尺寸一张缩略图，点一下就在画布上看它。A thumbnail for every size you export; click one to see it on the canvas.
  4. 调整范围Applies to拖动和缩放作用于所有尺寸、同方向的尺寸，或只这一个。A move or resize applies to every size, the sizes facing the same way, or this one alone.
  5. 你看着办 · 导出Surprise me · Export按空格随机抽六手；⌘E 打开导出。Space deals six hands; ⌘E opens Export.

EP.01尺寸Sizes

一张封面，六个尺寸。One cover, six sizes.

横版、竖版、方形各有自己的版式：16:9 左右排，3:4 上下排，方形用细通栏。在一个尺寸上拖动、缩放，按「调整范围」带到其他尺寸；导出时，档案要的尺寸一次全部写出来。Wide, tall and square each get a layout of their own — side by side at 16:9, stacked at 3:4, a slim banner on the square. Move or resize something on one size and Applies to carries it to the others; Export writes every size the profile wants in one go.

版式自己守着几条规矩，放进信息流就看得清。The layouts keep a few rules, so a cover reads in the feed as it comes.

14%

不被平台角标挡住Nothing under the badges

B站和 YouTube 会在横版封面底边两角盖上时长和播放量，标题、角标、频道名都待在这条带子上面；其他尺寸的文字也不进底部 14%。Bilibili and YouTube lay the duration and play count over a landscape cover's bottom corners; the title, tag and channel name stay above that strip. On other sizes, no words go into the bottom 14%.

14PX

最窄的信息流里也看得清Readable in the narrowest feed

标题框至少放得下两行字，按最窄的信息流算，字号也不小于 14px：抖音主页网格里 3:4 封面只有约 128px 宽，YouTube 侧栏里 16:9 封面约 168px（信息流宽度是估算的，还没在真机截图上量过）。The title's box leaves room for two lines at 14 px or more as the narrowest feed shows them — Douyin's grid shows a 3:4 cover about 128 px wide, YouTube's side list a 16:9 one about 168 px (feed widths are estimates, not yet measured on real phones).

6–8

一行 6～8 个字最好Six to eight characters a line

字越多，字号越小；有副标题时两者放不下，副标题先变小。The longer a line, the smaller it is set; when a subtitle and the title don't both fit, the subtitle shrinks first.

EP.02风格Looks

35 种风格，换一下就是另一张。35 looks. Switch, and it's another cover.

风格决定整张封面怎么画：背景、色块、标题和其他文字、频道图形一起换；内容、位置和图层都不变，随时切回来。下面每一张都是同一张封面，只换了风格。A look decides how the whole cover is drawn — the ground, the colour block, the lettering and the channel graphic change together, while the words, places and layers stay put. EP.03你看着办Surprise me

没灵感？按空格。Stuck? Press Space.

「你看着办」把这张封面怎么画重新随机一遍：风格和它的滑条、深浅底、标题的文字效果和变形、你加的图层摆在哪……一次抽六手，每手一个不同的风格。点一手放到画布上；不满意就再抽，或者撤销回去。Surprise me re-deals how the cover is drawn — the look and its sliders, the ground, the title's lettering and bend, where your own layers sit. It deals six hands at once, each in a different look; click one to put it on the canvas, deal again, or undo.

    EP.04字Lettering

    中文标题，按中文的规矩排。Chinese titles, set by Chinese rules.

    每一行按中文标题的排版规矩来：挨着汉字的西文和数字放大到和汉字一样高，标点按「开明式」挤压，行首行尾的标点不留空白。标题、副标题和你加的文字，都能单独换文字效果，再叠一种变形。Each line is set the way Chinese titles are: Latin letters and numerals beside the hanzi scaled to stand as tall, punctuation squeezed in the Kaiming style, an opening mark at the start of a line and a closing mark at its end losing their blank. The title, subtitle and any text you add can each take their own text effect, with a bend on top.

    变形 · 共 11 种，还能拖手柄手动调Bends · 11 of them, reshaped by hand with handles

    EP.05照片Photos

    抠出主体，让它挡住标题。Cut out the subject; let it cross the title.

    抠出的主体自动放在标题上面，像杂志封面那样挡住一部分字；再逐字或用画笔决定哪些字在前、哪些在后。抠图在本机完成，照片不上传。A cut-out lands above the title, covering part of it the way a magazine cover does — then choose letter by letter, or with a brush, which parts pass in front. Cutting out runs on your computer; the photo is never uploaded.

    • 本机抠图On-device cut-out · Mac 上用系统自带的 Apple Vision（macOS 14 及以上），通常一秒内；Windows 和 Linux 用开源模型 BiRefNet，第一次用前下载一次。Apple Vision on a Mac (macOS 14 and later), usually under a second; the open BiRefNet model on Windows and Linux, downloaded once before first use.
    • 穿插Weave · 自动（头在字前）、逐字选择，或者用画笔涂；贴纸和图形也能这样穿插。Automatic (the head in front), letter by letter, or painted with a brush; stickers and graphics can weave too.
    • 裁剪与描边Crop and outline · 常用比例，或用「自由」裁成任意形状；描边可选实线、撕纸边缘、线段。The usual ratios, or Free for any outline; outlines can be solid, torn paper or a dashed cut line.
    • AI 生成和重绘AI generate and repaint · 按描述生成一张照片，或把照片重画成另一种画风；一句话生成贴纸和立体图形。Generate a photo from a description, or repaint one in another style; one sentence gives you stickers and 3D graphics.

    AI 功能接你自己的模型服务：文本模型用 OpenAI 或 Anthropic 风格的接口（官方、中转或本地模型都行），图片模型支持 OpenAI gpt-image、Google Gemini 和火山方舟 Seedream。SmedCover 不自带模型，也不提供账号，费用由你的服务商收取。The AI features use your own model service: an OpenAI- or Anthropic-style API for text (official, a relay or a local model), and OpenAI gpt-image, Google Gemini or Volcengine Seedream for images. SmedCover ships no model and no account; your provider bills you.

    EP.06频道与批量Channels and batches

    每个栏目一种颜色，一张表出一整批。A colour for every series, a whole batch from one sheet.

    档案是一个品牌的全部设置：品牌名、频道、尺寸和一套默认设计，可以存好几套。频道是品牌下的栏目，有自己的名称、主色和图形；每个频道有自己的那张封面，互不影响。A profile holds a brand — its name, channels, sizes and a default design — and you can keep several. A channel is a series under it, with its own name, colour and graphic, and its own cover, untouched by the others.

    EP.07Agent 与命令行Agents and the command line
